Dugsi and Quran Classes for Columbus Families

Central Ohio is home to the second-largest Somali community in the United States, with about 27,700 people of Somali ancestry in the Columbus metro, alongside Ethiopian, Oromo and Kenyan families (U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024).

Ta'leem Quran Academy gives each learner, child or adult, a clear Quran goal, a teacher matched to their level, and regular progress updates.

Weekday Quran Support Next to Your Masjid's Weekend Dugsi

Many Columbus children already go to a weekend dugsi at their masjid. Ta'leem can add short 1-on-1 lessons on school nights, so reading, memorization or revision keeps moving between weekends instead of starting over each Saturday.

Families who aren't in a weekend program can use Ta'leem on its own. The recommended plan is four 45-minute classes a week, and smaller plans are available.

Classes Scheduled Around the Ohio School Day

Lessons are booked in Eastern Time around school, work and family, whether that means after school, evenings or weekends. Class times are built around the student and the family's schedule, and can change when your schedule does.

Online anywhere in Central Ohio

Learn from home in Columbus, the Northland area, the suburbs, or anywhere else in Ohio.

In person in Columbus

In-person classes can be arranged where the student is. Tell us your part of town and the learner's age, and we'll set up the arrangements and schedule with you before you enroll.

Programs for Every Level

  • Hingaad / Arabic letters: sounds, letters and joining for beginners of any age.
  • Quran reading: accuracy, fluency and confidence.
  • Memorization (xifdi): new memorization with proper review.
  • Revision (muraajaco): strengthening what was memorized before.
  • Islamic studies: on its own or alongside Quran.
