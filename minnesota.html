Dugsi and Quran Classes for Minnesota Families

Ta'leem Quran Academy helps learners of every age in Minneapolis, St. Paul, the Twin Cities and across Minnesota build a clear Quran goal, with the right teacher, flexible scheduling and progress updates.

Learn online from anywhere in the state, or ask about in-person classes and peer groups near you. Somali, Oromo, Kenyan and all East African families are welcome.

Quran Learning That Fits Minnesota Family Life

Between school, work and family, it can be hard to make time for dugsi. Ta'leem builds class times around the student and the family's schedule, so learning keeps moving through busy weeks.

Most students learn 1-on-1, so the Quran teacher understands their level and needs. Attendance and progress are tracked, and adult students or parents receive updates on what was covered and what to practice next.

Online or In Person in Minnesota

Online classes anywhere in Minnesota

Online Quran classes work from home in Minneapolis, St. Paul, the suburbs and Greater Minnesota. You choose the weekly hours and times that suit your family.

In-person classes and peer groups

Prefer learning face to face, or want your child to learn alongside others? Tell us your city and the learner's age, and we'll confirm the local class arrangements and schedule before you enroll.

What You Can Learn

  • Arabic letters / hingaad: letters, sounds, joining and foundation, for beginners of any age.
  • Quran reading: accuracy, fluency and confidence.
  • Memorization: new memorization with proper review.
  • Revision: strengthening what was memorized before.
  • Extra support / 1-on-1: more attention and structure.
  • Islamic studies: on its own or alongside Quran learning.
